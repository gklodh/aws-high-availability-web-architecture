Questions

What is an AI agent?

Software that takes a goal, decides the steps, uses your systems to carry them out and checks its own work. Unlike a fixed script, it can handle variation, and we limit what it is allowed to do.

How long does a first project take?

Most first agents reach production in eight to twelve weeks, starting with human approval on every action.

Will agents replace my team?

In our projects, agents take over the repetitive steps and people move to exceptions, judgement calls and customer conversations. Approval stays with your team.

What if an agent makes a mistake?

Limits and approval rules contain the impact, the audit trail shows what happened, and the evaluation suite is updated so the same mistake is caught next time.

Do you work with our existing tools?

Yes. We connect to your current systems and add agents alongside them rather than asking you to replace anything.
