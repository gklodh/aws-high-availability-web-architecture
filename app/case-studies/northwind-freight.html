Case studies / Logistics / Northwind Freight

Dispatch that re-plans itself

A regional carrier replaced manual morning re-planning with a team of monitoring and routing agents supervised by dispatchers.

ClientNorthwind Freight
SectorLogistics
Scale1,400 vehicles
Project length9 weeks
41%fewer late deliveries
3.5 hsaved per dispatcher each day
9 weeksfrom kickoff to full rollout

The problem

Dispatchers spent the first hours of every shift rebuilding routes after cancellations, weather and driver changes. Customers were often told about delays hours after they were known internally.

Services: Agent design, integrations, evaluation

What the agents do

  1. A monitoring agent reads carrier, weather and traffic feeds every five minutes.
  2. A planning agent proposes new routes and load assignments whenever a delay becomes likely.
  3. Dispatchers approve changes in one step, and customers are notified automatically.

How the project ran

Weeks 1–2: MapShadowed three depots and documented the 31 decisions dispatchers make daily.
Weeks 3–5: PrototypeReplayed six months of historical disruptions through the planning agent in a sandbox.
Weeks 6–7: DeployLive in two depots with approval required on every change.
Weeks 8–9: OperateApproval relaxed for low-risk reroutes; rollout to all depots.
