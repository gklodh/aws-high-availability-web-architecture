Case studies / Retail / Brightcart

Customer support that resolves, not deflects

An online retailer handled a doubling of seasonal ticket volume with agents that can act on orders, not just answer questions.

ClientBrightcart
SectorRetail
Scale180k tickets/month
Project length8 weeks
68%of tickets resolved end to end
52%lower cost per ticket
4.7/5average customer rating

The problem

Ticket volume doubled in peak season. The existing chatbot answered questions but could not issue refunds or fix orders, so most chats ended up with a person.

Services: Agent design, integrations, evaluation

What the agents do

  1. A support agent reads the order, shipping and policy data before replying.
  2. It can refund, reship or rebook within limits set by the support team.
  3. Anything outside those limits goes to a human with a drafted reply.

How the project ran

Weeks 1–2: MapClassified 20,000 tickets to find the 12 request types that make up 80% of volume.
Weeks 3–5: PrototypeBuilt tool access to orders and payments with strict refund limits.
Weeks 6–7: DeployLaunched on email, then chat, behind a 10% traffic split.
Week 8: OperateFull traffic before the peak season began.
