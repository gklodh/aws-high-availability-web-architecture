Case studies / Healthcare / Halcyon Health

Prior authorisations in hours, not days

A hospital network cut the time to prepare insurance approvals by having agents assemble every packet for clinician review.

ClientHalcyon Health
SectorHealthcare
Scale6 hospitals
Project length12 weeks
6 hmedian turnaround, down from 4 days
63%fewer first-pass denials
100%packets reviewed by a clinician

The problem

Nurses assembled clinical notes and payer forms by hand. Each denial sent the request back to the start, and patients waited days for approved procedures.

Services: Agent design, compliance, integrations

What the agents do

  1. An intake agent extracts diagnoses, codes and history from the patient record.
  2. A payer-rules agent matches each request to the insurer's current criteria.
  3. A clinician reviews and signs the complete packet before anything is sent.

How the project ran

Weeks 1–3: MapReviewed 400 past requests and catalogued the top denial reasons.
Weeks 4–7: PrototypeBuilt the extraction and rules agents and scored them against clinician-labelled cases.
Weeks 8–10: DeployPilot in one cardiology unit with full clinician review.
Weeks 11–12: OperateExpanded to six hospitals with weekly quality reviews.
