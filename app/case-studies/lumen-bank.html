Case studies / Finance / Lumen Bank

Business onboarding without the queue

A commercial bank automated document checks and screening for new business accounts while keeping analysts in charge of every exception.

ClientLumen Bank
SectorFinance
Scale2,300 accounts/month
Project length14 weeks
12 minaverage check time, down from 3 days
78%of cases fully automated
0policy exceptions in audit

The problem

New business accounts waited up to a week for document checks, and analysts re-keyed the same data into three systems.

Services: Agent design, governance, integrations

What the agents do

  1. A document agent reads, validates and cross-checks submitted files.
  2. A screening agent runs sanctions and adverse-media checks and writes its rationale.
  3. Only unclear cases reach an analyst, with the evidence already attached.

How the project ran

Weeks 1–3: MapMapped the onboarding policy and agreed approval limits with compliance.
Weeks 4–8: PrototypeTested on anonymised historical applications; every decision logged with reasoning.
Weeks 9–12: DeployShadow mode alongside analysts, then live for low-risk entities.
Weeks 13–14: OperateInternal audit sign-off and extension to higher-risk segments.
“Every decision comes with its reasoning, which is exactly what our auditors ask for.”Chief Compliance Officer, Lumen Bank
