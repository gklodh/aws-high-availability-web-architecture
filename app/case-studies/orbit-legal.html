Contract review at scale

A law firm's commercial team compared every vendor contract against its playbook in minutes, leaving lawyers to decide on the judgement calls.

ClientOrbit Legal
SectorLegal

The problem

Lawyers reviewed every vendor contract line by line, so smaller deals were signed unreviewed or waited for weeks.

Services: Agent design, playbook modelling, evaluation

What the agents do

  1. A clause agent maps each contract against the firm's negotiation playbook.
  2. A redline agent proposes edits with its reasoning and fallback positions.
  3. A lawyer accepts, edits or rejects each suggestion inside Word.

How the project ran

Weeks 1–2: MapTurned the playbook into structured, testable rules with senior partners.
Weeks 3–4: PrototypeBlind comparison against lawyer reviews of 150 contracts.
Weeks 5–6: DeployWord add-in rolled out to one practice group.
Week 7: OperateExtended to all commercial teams with a monthly review.
